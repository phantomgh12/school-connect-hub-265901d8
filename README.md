# School Connect Hub

Build a modern, full-stack, responsive social networking platform called SchoolConnect. It is designed for students from Primary School, Junior High School (JHS), Senior High School (SHS), Technical/Vocational Schools, and alumni to connect, make friends, chat, share memories, collaborate academically, and stay informed.

The platform should feel like a combination of Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Snapchat, Discord, and LinkedIn for students, with a clean, modern UI, smooth animations, glassmorphism, dark/light mode, and excellent performance on both mobile and desktop.

Project Goals

Create a safe, engaging, feature-rich student social network where users can:

Meet students from different schools

Connect with classmates and alumni

Make friends

Chat privately or in groups

Share posts, photos, and videos

Upload disappearing stories

Discover educational news

Participate in school communities

Build academic and personal profiles

The website should support thousands of users and be built using scalable architecture.

Final Goal

Build SchoolConnect as a secure, scalable social networking platform for students and alumni across different schools and educational levels. The platform should emphasize meaningful connections, collaboration, and positive community interactions while protecting users' privacy and providing administrators with robust moderation tools. It should feel fast, intuitive, engaging, and production-ready.
